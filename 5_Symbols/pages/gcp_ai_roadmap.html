Professional Machine Learning Engineer Certification

Professional ML Engineer

🎯 Why Google Cloud ML Engineer

Google invented TensorFlow, leads in TPU hardware, and runs one of the world's largest AI research organisations (DeepMind, Google Brain). The Professional ML Engineer cert validates expert-level ability to design, build, and productionise ML models on Google Cloud's Vertex AI platform.

"The most technically demanding cloud ML certification — it expects you to know the full ML lifecycle, not just click a managed service."

🧩 Core Skill Domains

🗂️

Frame ML Problems

Translate business requirements into ML problem definitions: classification, regression, ranking, generation.

  • Problem framing & success metrics
  • Data readiness assessment
  • Build vs buy decisions
📊

Architect ML Solutions

Design scalable, reliable ML systems on Google Cloud using Vertex AI, BigQuery ML, and data pipelines.

  • Vertex AI Architecture
  • BigQuery ML for SQL-native models
  • Feature Store design
🏋️

Prepare & Process Data

Data ingestion, validation, transformation, and feature engineering at scale using Dataflow and Dataprep.

  • Cloud Dataflow (Apache Beam)
  • TFX data validation
  • Vertex AI Feature Store
🧪

Develop ML Models

Train custom models with TensorFlow/PyTorch on Vertex AI Training; tune hyperparameters at scale.

  • Vertex AI Training (custom jobs)
  • Hyperparameter tuning (Vizier)
  • AutoML vs custom training trade-offs
🚀

Deploy & Serve Models

Deploy models to production endpoints, manage versions, and optimise for latency and throughput.

  • Vertex AI Endpoints
  • TF Serving & ONNX export
  • Online vs batch prediction
🔁

Automate & Monitor MLOps

Build CI/CD ML pipelines, track experiments, detect drift, and retrain automatically with Vertex AI Pipelines.

  • Vertex AI Pipelines (Kubeflow)
  • Model monitoring & drift detection
  • CT/CD for ML systems
